A PERSON BEHIND THE PENCIL

Learning to see
the world differently.

I’m Suchir, an independent artist developing a personal practice through sketching, visual observation, and experimentation. My work is guided by a continuing interest in expression, proportion, form, and the small details that give an image its character. Each study is an opportunity to refine my eye, test an approach, and build a more deliberate creative process.

Suchir Gallery is a curated record of that process: a place to present selected work, document ongoing exploration, and share the ideas that shape each new piece. The collection continues to evolve as I learn, practise, and explore new directions.
