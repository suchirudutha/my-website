DIFFERENT WAYS TO MAKE A MARK

One pencil.
Many possibilities.

Drawing styles each have their own rhythm. Some exaggerate, some observe, and some simplify the world into its essential shapes. Explore a few starting points below.

01 / FEATURED↗
EXPRESSION · CHARACTER

Caricature drawing

Play with recognisable features, expressive shapes, and personality. Caricature is about capturing the character of a subject, not simply copying every detail.

02 / OBSERVE
LINE · DETAIL

Line drawing

Build an image with contours, marks, and the spaces between them. A confident line can suggest volume without filling every surface.

Form & shading
